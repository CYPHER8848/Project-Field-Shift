# Story outline — Four Seasons of Amma's Field

1. **The inheritance (1990):** Introduce the family field, routines and knowledge passed between generations.
2. **A familiar rhythm (2005):** Show how the seasonal calendar and crop choices become familiar.
3. **The present field (2026):** Introduce changing weather patterns and the limits of relying on the old rhythm.
4. **Three possible paths:** Compare a baseline approach, a resilience-focused rotation and an income-prioritising option.
5. **A future to test (2040):** Explore scenario outcomes as possibilities, not predictions.
6. **The farmer's decision:** Return agency to the farmer and local expertise; data supports judgement rather than replacing it.

## Glossary
- NDVI: satellite-derived vegetation greenness indicator.
- Evapotranspiration: water transferred from land and plants to the atmosphere.
- Soil wetness: a moisture-related indicator; POWER wetness variables are not volumetric soil moisture.
- Root-zone wetness: modelled wetness for a deeper soil layer.
- Land surface temperature: temperature of the land surface, not air temperature.
- Growing degree days: heat accumulation used to describe crop development.
- Scenario: a conditional exploration, not a forecast.
- Spatial resolution: ground area represented by a pixel or grid cell.
- Ground validation: comparison of remote/modelled estimates with local measurements.
